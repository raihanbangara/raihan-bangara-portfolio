How it worked
A calendar, planned day by day

I owned the content calendar and the full editorial pipeline across four platforms: planning the schedule, producing daily content, and getting leadership sign-off before anything published.

Testimonials, cut for each channel

Customer testimonials became carousels designed in Canva, adapted to what each platform's audience actually stops for rather than reposted identically everywhere.

Research that fed the content

I built a competitor intelligence repository covering 15 companies, surfacing differentiators, top-dollar accounts, and positioning gaps. Audience segmentation from that work drove the content optimization behind the engagement lift.

The client

Verix is a verification platform — blockchain- and AI-backed digital awards, credentials, and certificates that people can share and anyone can authenticate.

A category that has to explain itself before it can sell, which made social the proving ground. I built the posts from scratch — writing, designing, and scheduling every asset across four channels, and grounding each one in competitor and audience research so the storytelling stayed sharp.

Also

Placed second company-wide in the Verix innovation challenge, pitching an app engagement strategy against cross-functional teams.
