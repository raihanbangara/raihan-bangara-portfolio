Case study 02 · Sports marketing · 2025

JioStar

Integrated marketing briefs and campaign strategy for Premier League programming and other live sports properties across the Star Sports network.

RoleSports marketing
WhereMumbai, India
PropertiesSix live sports
OutcomeConcept aired
The work

Seven marketing briefs and campaign decks across six live sports properties, written for an audience that watches on national television.

I sat in on strategy meetings and turned them into briefs covering audience engagement and seasonal campaign planning for Wimbledon, the US Open, the Premier League, SA20, Pro Kabaddi, and the India versus England test series. Each one had to hold up in front of senior leadership, and several were later shared with external league partners.

Premier League Wimbledon US Open SA20 Pro Kabaddi India v England
The teaser
Selected concept

My concept was chosen for the Premier League teaser ad and aired to hundreds of millions of viewers on cable TV across the country.

I pitched multiple teaser directions for the campaign. One was selected from my submission, produced, and aired on the Star Sports network during marquee programming. I also presented strategic recommendations to senior leadership and external league stakeholders, which shaped how the final campaign ran.

What I took from it
Writing for scale

A brief for national broadcast has to be unambiguous. There is no second draft once the spot is cut.

Reading the room

Presenting to leadership and to league partners are two different pitches for the same idea.

Season shape

Sports marketing runs on fixture calendars. Planning against them is most of the job.
